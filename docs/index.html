A Tarkatan's Tale

Leena is a Tarkatan adopted by Hotaru in Orderrealm as a social experiment. She escapes his grip, is trained by Bo-Rai-Cho in Outworld, and grows into a fighter caught between rivalries and unlikely bonds — carving her own path toward Edenia.

An original story with characters inspired by Mortal Kombat lore.

Roster

Faces you'll meet on Leena's journey across the realms.

  • Leena

    Tarkatan protagonist, raised far from home and fighting to define her own fate.

  • Hotaru

    Orderrealm enforcer whose rigid sense of order shapes Leena's early years.

  • Darrius

    Resistance fighter who pushes back against the established order.

  • Dairou

    A soldier of the undesirables, moving through the same resistance circles.

  • Bo-Rai-Cho

    Outworld master who takes Leena under his wing and trains her to fight.

  • Li-Mei

    Fellow student and fierce rival, forever testing Leena's resolve.

  • Baraka

    Tarkatan warrior whose kinship with Leena runs deeper than either expects.

  • Onaga

    The Dragon King — an ancient, looming power over the story's events.
